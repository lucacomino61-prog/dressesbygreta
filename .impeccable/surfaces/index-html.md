# Home page surface brief, Dresses by Greta

Scope: the single route `/` (index.html) plus the fitting-room dialog and the menu, search and list drawers it opens. Visitor mode: Persuade, carried by one Experience moment (the fitting room). Audience: young women in Tirana and their mothers, arriving from the Instagram bio link on a phone. Job: understand the boutique in one glance, browse every dress, put one on herself, message the boutique. Proof: her own reflection wearing the dress. Constraints: no prices, hours, rules, sizes or per-dress status exist; Albanian first with an English toggle; GSAP is the only scheduler; camera frames never leave the device.

Client pin (2026-09-16, Luca): follow vivetofficial.com (Shopify Horizon) and copy its design, menu included. The pin overrides the earlier dark graphite world in every visual dimension; the rail, catalog and fitting room keep their mechanics under the new skin.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A white Helvetica fashion store in the reference's grammar, with one thing the reference cannot offer: the visitor tries the dress on herself. The category default the page refuses is the pink boutique grid and the fake 3D spinning dress; it also refuses the reference's emptiness by keeping a real catalog behind the photo-only home screen.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, black ink, 12px uppercase chrome with 0.02em tracking, 14px body, square corners everywhere, filled black buttons with white uppercase labels, 1px hairlines. Photographs are the only colour. The scan-bar print (1px black line, clip-path by --p) is kept from the earlier world as the reveal grammar for every photograph. Drawers slide in as white panels over a 20% backdrop.

STORY: She lands on one photograph with DRESSES BY GRETA pinned in the centre and a single "Provoje". She scrolls: the featured gowns open on the rail one by one, then every dress in the grid. She taps a dress; the fitting room opens with that dress standing on the gate; she turns the camera on and sees the dress on herself in a mirror, freezes, flicks through the rail, adds the ones she likes to her list, copies the list and sends it to Greta on Instagram.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Transparent header, 49px on phones and 54px from 1024px: MENU (phones) or the inline category list (desktop) on the left, DRESSES BY GRETA centred, EN / KËRKO / LISTA on the right (phones show only LISTA). Below it the photograph fills 100svh under a 15% overlay and a 120px top gradient; the wordmark sits in the exact centre at clamp(2rem, 5.6vw, 5.5rem) on one line from 768px; an outline "Provoje" button sits bottom-centre 28px (phones) or 40px (desktop) from the edge. Nothing else.

FORM: The reference store (client-pinned), fused with the garment-rail topology assigned by Impeccable seed 48f6c139 (the featured gowns as sticky sleeves) and the scan-bar grammar of the judge-panel winner. Challengers from the roll are recorded in the earlier contract; the pin settles the material.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
